Job Interview Tips: How to Prepare and Get Hired

Getting invited to a job interview is an important step towards employment. An interview gives an employer a chance to learn more about you, your experience and your skills.

Whether you are applying for your first job or looking for a new opportunity in Eswatini or South Africa, good preparation can help you perform with confidence.

Remember: You do not need to know every answer perfectly. Be honest, stay calm and explain your answers clearly.

1. Research the Company

Before the interview, learn about the organisation. Find out what it does, its services and the position you are applying for.

  • Visit the company's official website.
  • Understand the job description.
  • Learn about its products or services.
  • Prepare questions about the role.

2. Dress Professionally

Choose clean, neat and appropriate clothing. Your appearance should show that you take the opportunity seriously.

3. Arrive on Time

Plan your journey in advance. If the interview is in person, try to arrive about 10 to 15 minutes early. For online interviews, test your internet connection, microphone and camera beforehand.

4. Practise Common Interview Questions

Question 1: Tell us about yourself.

Sample answer:

My name is [Your Name]. I am a motivated and hardworking person with an interest in developing my skills. I enjoy learning new things and working with others. I am excited about this opportunity because it will allow me to contribute and grow professionally.

Question 2: Why do you want this job?

Sample answer:

I am interested in this position because it matches my skills and career goals. I believe I can contribute positively to the organisation while learning and developing professionally.

Question 3: What are your strengths?

Sample answer:

My strengths include communication, teamwork and time management. I am also willing to learn and take responsibility for my work.

Question 4: What is your weakness?

Sample answer:

Sometimes I spend too much time checking my work because I want it to be accurate. I am learning to balance attention to detail with working efficiently.

Question 5: Why should we hire you?

Sample answer:

I am responsible, hardworking and eager to contribute. I am willing to learn, follow instructions and work towards the organisation's goals.

These are examples only. Adapt your answers to your real experience, qualifications and the position.

5. What If You Have No Work Experience?

Do not panic if this is your first interview. Talk about your education, school projects, volunteering, practical skills and willingness to learn.

Employers may also be interested in your attitude, communication and ability to work with others.

6. Questions You Can Ask the Employer

  • What does a normal working day look like?
  • What skills are most important for this position?
  • Will training be provided?
  • What are the next steps in the recruitment process?

7. Mistakes to Avoid During an Interview

  • Arriving late without communicating.
  • Giving dishonest answers.
  • Interrupting the interviewer.
  • Speaking negatively about previous employers.
  • Failing to understand the job you applied for.
  • Using your phone during the interview.

Final Interview Checklist

  • I researched the organisation.
  • I understand the job requirements.
  • I prepared answers to common questions.
  • I have my CV and requested documents ready.
  • I know the interview location or online link.
  • I am ready to ask relevant questions.
Final advice: Believe in your abilities. Prepare honestly, communicate clearly and remember that every interview is also a learning experience.
