Bursaries and Scholarships in Eswatini and South Africa

Are you a student looking for financial assistance to continue your education? Bursaries and scholarships can help reduce the cost of studying and make higher education more accessible.

This guide explains how to find education funding opportunities in Eswatini and South Africa, what documents you may need and how to submit a strong application.

Important: Every funding programme has its own eligibility requirements, closing dates and conditions. Always read the official announcement before applying.

1. What Is a Bursary?

A bursary is financial assistance given to a student to help pay for education expenses. Depending on the programme, it may cover tuition fees, accommodation, books, meals or other approved costs.

2. What Is a Scholarship?

A scholarship is financial support awarded to students based on criteria such as academic performance, leadership, talent, financial circumstances or a particular field of study.

3. Where to Find Bursaries in Eswatini

Students in Eswatini can investigate funding opportunities from different organisations, including:

  • Government education funding programmes.
  • Universities and colleges.
  • Commercial banks and financial institutions.
  • Private companies and corporate foundations.
  • Non-governmental organisations.
  • International education and development organisations.

Check official announcements from the relevant institutions and education authorities. Ask your university or college financial aid office about available funding.

4. Where to Find Bursaries in South Africa

South African students can investigate opportunities from government departments, universities, companies and education funding organisations.

  • National and provincial government departments.
  • University financial aid offices.
  • Corporate bursary programmes.
  • Professional and industry organisations.
  • Foundations and charitable organisations.
Official funding information:

Visit the South African National Student Financial Aid Scheme (NSFAS) website to check its funding information and eligibility rules.

Visit the official NSFAS website

5. Common Bursary Requirements

Requirements differ between funding organisations. Some common requirements include:

  • Proof of acceptance or registration at an institution.
  • Academic results or transcripts.
  • Proof of identity.
  • Proof of household income, if required.
  • A motivation letter.
  • Proof of residence, if requested.
  • Evidence of the qualification or course being studied.

6. Documents to Prepare

Prepare a folder containing commonly requested documents:

  • Certified identity document or passport, if requested.
  • Latest academic results.
  • Admission or registration letter.
  • Parent or guardian income documents, if required.
  • Proof of residence, if required.
  • Motivation letter.
Keep digital copies of your documents on your phone. Make sure they are readable and follow the file format and size requirements in the application.

7. How to Write a Bursary Motivation Letter

A motivation letter explains why you need financial assistance and why you deserve to be considered.

Example motivation letter:

Dear Bursary Selection Committee,

I am writing to express my interest in applying for your bursary programme. I am a motivated student who is committed to completing my education and building a successful career.

Financial assistance would help me cover my educational expenses and focus on my studies. I am dedicated to working hard and making the most of this opportunity.

Thank you for considering my application.

Kind regards,
[Your Full Name]

Personalise the letter with your real circumstances, academic goals and the specific bursary requirements.

8. How to Apply for a Bursary

  1. Find a genuine funding opportunity.
  2. Check whether you meet the eligibility requirements.
  3. Read the application instructions carefully.
  4. Prepare all required documents.
  5. Complete the application form accurately.
  6. Submit before the closing date.
  7. Save your application confirmation.
  8. Monitor your email for updates.

9. Tips for Getting Your Application Right

  • Apply for funding related to your course.
  • Submit complete and accurate information.
  • Write a clear motivation letter.
  • Apply before the deadline.
  • Keep copies of submitted documents.
  • Check your email regularly.

10. Beware of Bursary Scams

Unfortunately, fake bursary advertisements can target students who urgently need financial assistance.

  • Never pay a stranger to guarantee a bursary.
  • Verify the organisation through its official website.
  • Be careful with suspicious application links.
  • Do not share passwords or banking PINs.
  • Check whether the funding announcement is genuine.
Final advice: Start searching early, prepare your documents and apply only through verified application channels. Missing one deadline should not stop you from looking for another opportunity.
