Graduate Internships in Eswatini and South Africa

Have you recently completed your studies and are looking for work experience? A graduate internship can help you develop professional skills, gain workplace experience and understand how your chosen industry operates.

Many organisations offer internship opportunities to students, graduates and people entering the job market. This guide explains how to find and apply for them.

Important: Internship opportunities, qualifications, age limits and application deadlines differ between employers. Always read the official vacancy advertisement before applying.

1. What Is a Graduate Internship?

A graduate internship is a temporary work experience opportunity designed to help graduates develop practical skills in a professional environment.

Depending on the organisation, interns may receive workplace training, mentorship and a monthly stipend. Payment and benefits are not guaranteed, so check the advertisement carefully.

2. Where to Find Internships in Eswatini

Start by checking the official websites and recruitment announcements of organisations in Eswatini.

  • Government ministries and departments.
  • Commercial banks and financial institutions.
  • Telecommunications companies.
  • Private companies and major employers.
  • Non-governmental organisations.
  • Universities and other educational institutions.

You can also check career offices at educational institutions for internship announcements.

3. Where to Find Internships in South Africa

South Africa has internship opportunities across government departments, private companies and professional organisations.

  • National and provincial government departments.
  • Public entities and state-owned organisations.
  • Banks and financial institutions.
  • Technology and engineering companies.
  • Graduate development programmes.
  • Non-profit organisations.
Useful official source:

South African government vacancies are published through the Department of Public Service and Administration.

Visit the DPSA official website

4. Common Internship Requirements

Requirements depend on the internship and the employer. Some common requirements include:

  • A recognised diploma or degree.
  • A relevant field of study.
  • An updated CV.
  • Academic transcripts.
  • Certified qualifications, when requested.
  • A motivation letter, when required.
  • Proof of identity or work eligibility, when requested.

Some internships accept applicants who have completed high school, while others require a university qualification.

5. Documents to Prepare Before Applying

  • Professional CV.
  • Certified copies of qualifications, if requested.
  • Academic transcripts.
  • Motivation letter, if required.
  • Contact details for references, if requested.

6. How to Apply for an Internship

  1. Find an internship that matches your qualifications.
  2. Read the full vacancy advertisement.
  3. Prepare your CV and required documents.
  4. Write a motivation letter if requested.
  5. Submit your application using the official method.
  6. Keep a copy of your application.
  7. Check your email for recruitment updates.

7. Tips for Getting Selected

  • Apply for internships related to your studies.
  • Keep your CV clear and professional.
  • Highlight relevant projects and practical skills.
  • Show willingness to learn.
  • Apply before the closing date.
  • Prepare for possible interviews.

8. Can You Apply Without Work Experience?

Yes. Many graduate internships are designed for people who are just starting their careers.

If you have no formal employment history, highlight academic projects, volunteering, leadership activities, computer skills and other relevant experience.

9. Beware of Internship Scams

Be careful when applying online. Genuine employers should provide clear information about their organisation, requirements and application process.

  • Do not pay someone who guarantees an internship.
  • Verify the employer's official website.
  • Be careful with requests for sensitive personal details.
  • Do not trust suspicious recruitment messages.
Final advice: Do not wait until you graduate to start preparing. Build your CV, develop useful skills and regularly check official internship announcements.
