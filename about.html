About Us

Welcome to Eswatini Jobs Hub, an independent online platform dedicated to helping job seekers discover employment opportunities and useful career information.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make employment information easier to find and access. We want to help people discover opportunities that can support their careers and future.

We focus primarily on opportunities in Eswatini and South Africa, while sharing useful resources for job seekers at different stages of their careers.

What We Do

Eswatini Jobs Hub provides information and resources covering:

  • Job vacancies in Eswatini.
  • Employment opportunities in South Africa.
  • Graduate programmes and internships.
  • Bursaries and educational opportunities.
  • CV writing and job application guidance.
  • Interview preparation and career advice.

Who We Serve

Our platform is intended for students, graduates, experienced professionals and anyone looking for employment opportunities. Whether you are searching for your first job or considering your next career move, we aim to make useful information easier to discover.

Our Commitment to Visitors

We aim to share information in a clear, accessible and useful way. We encourage every visitor to check vacancy details carefully and apply through official employer websites or authorised application channels.

Your safety matters.

Never pay money to someone who promises to guarantee you a job, interview or recruitment opportunity. Always verify employers and application links before sharing personal information.

Important Disclaimer

Eswatini Jobs Hub is an independent job information platform. We are not a government department, recruitment agency or employer. We are not officially affiliated with the organisations whose opportunities may be shared on this website.

We do not guarantee employment, vacancy authenticity or continued availability. Job seekers should verify all information directly with the relevant employer.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a useful online destination that makes employment information, career guidance and educational opportunities easier to discover across Eswatini and the region.
