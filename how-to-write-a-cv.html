How to Write a Professional CV

A Curriculum Vitae (CV) is a document that tells employers about your education, work experience, skills and achievements. A good CV helps an employer understand why you may be suitable for a job.

Remember: Your CV should be clear, honest, professional and easy to read. Always adapt it to the job you are applying for.

1. Add Your Contact Information

Start your CV with your basic contact details:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Professional email address
  • Town or city
  • Professional LinkedIn profile, if available

Avoid unnecessary personal information such as your bank details or identification number.

2. Write a Short Personal Profile

This is a short introduction explaining who you are, what you can offer and what type of opportunity you want.

Example:

Motivated and hardworking recent graduate with strong communication, teamwork and problem-solving skills. Eager to learn, gain experience and contribute positively to an organisation.

3. Include Your Education

List your education, starting with your most recent qualification.

Example:

High School Certificate
Example Secondary School
Completed: 2025

Include your actual qualifications, institution names and completion dates.

4. Add Your Work Experience

Include previous jobs, internships, volunteering or relevant work experience. Explain your responsibilities and achievements.

If you have never worked before, you can include school projects, volunteering, leadership roles and practical experience.

5. List Your Skills

Include skills that are relevant to the job.

  • Communication
  • Teamwork
  • Computer literacy
  • Time management
  • Problem-solving
  • Customer service

Only include skills you genuinely have.

6. Add References

Some employers request references. Ask permission before listing someone as a reference and provide their correct contact details when required.

7. Common CV Mistakes to Avoid

  • Spelling and grammar mistakes.
  • Providing false qualifications or experience.
  • Using an unprofessional email address.
  • Making the CV difficult to read.
  • Sending the wrong CV for the job.
  • Including unnecessary personal information.

Final CV Checklist

  • My contact details are correct.
  • My education information is accurate.
  • My skills match the job requirements.
  • I checked spelling and grammar.
  • My CV is saved as a PDF when requested.
Job seeker tip: Always follow the employer's application instructions. Some employers may require a specific CV format or supporting documents.
