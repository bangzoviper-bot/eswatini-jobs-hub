Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Welcome to Eswatini Jobs Hub. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you visit our website and how that information is handled.

1. About Eswatini Jobs Hub

Eswatini Jobs Hub is an independent online platform that shares job opportunities, internships, bursaries and career information to help visitors discover opportunities.

2. Information We Collect

Visitors can browse publicly available job listings without creating an account or submitting personal information.

When you contact us by email, we may receive information such as your email address, name and the contents of your message.

Our website hosting provider and other technical services may process basic technical information, such as browser details, device information and server logs, to operate and protect the website.

3. How We Use Information

Information received may be used to:

  • Respond to questions and enquiries.
  • Improve the website and its services.
  • Investigate technical problems.
  • Protect the website against misuse and unauthorised access.

4. Third-Party Services

Eswatini Jobs Hub uses third-party services to support its operation. For example, Supabase is used to store and manage job listing information and administrator authentication.

GitHub Pages is used to host this website. These providers may process technical information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Job application links may take you to external websites. We do not control those websites or their privacy practices. Please review their privacy policies before submitting personal information.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website does not currently intentionally use advertising cookies. However, hosting providers, authentication services or other third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for their functionality.

If analytics or advertising services are introduced in the future, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant data practices.

6. Advertising

If we introduce advertising services such as Google AdSense, third-party advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to display and measure advertisements, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements.

This policy will be updated when advertising services are added to the website.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through our website. However, no online service or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for people seeking job, internship, bursary and career information. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through public job browsing.

9. Your Choices and Privacy Requests

If you have questions about information you have submitted to us, or wish to request its correction or deletion where applicable, please contact us.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services or legal requirements change. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised update date.
